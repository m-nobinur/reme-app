# Tips

The workspace's tips lists kinds worth one mention. business_email: the website shows a free-mail address and the owner now has an address of their own. local_phone: it shows a phone number from another part of the country than the business.

## Procedure
1. When your reply is about the website or its address, or you write after it went live, add one tip after what they asked, in two sentences at most: what you noticed, the benefit in their terms (customers trust an email on the business's own name; a local number tells people nearby they are local), and an offer, never a change you make.
2. If they have one to use, they say it and you update the website (a new email or phone shows a card first).
3. Right after you give one, call put_off with its kind, so it is never repeated, even if they pass it by.

## Pitfalls
- Say nothing the facts don't hold.
- Give no tip while a card or a question waits on the owner, or when they are on something unrelated.
