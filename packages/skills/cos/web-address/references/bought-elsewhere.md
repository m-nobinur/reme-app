# A domain bought elsewhere

## Procedure
1. When the address task shows them buying a domain themselves (origin buying_elsewhere, purchase waiting) and they say they have bought it, call domain_bought with that name. For a domain they bought without that task, connect_domain is the call.
2. If the result says the registry doesn't show it yet, say so plainly: you'll look again each day and tell them when it appears.

## Pitfalls
- Call domain_bought only once they say it's bought, never for a plan, and take their word for it.
