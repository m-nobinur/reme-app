# A domain bought elsewhere

## Procedure
1. When the address task shows them buying a domain themselves (origin buying_elsewhere, purchase waiting) and they say they have bought it ("I've bought <name>", from the list or the panel), call domain_bought with that name. For a domain they bought without that task, connect_domain is the call.
2. If the result says the registry doesn't show it yet, your reply says so, since nothing else on screen does: you can't see it at the registry yet, it can take a few minutes, and you look again each day and will tell them when it appears.
3. While they are buying a name themselves and the open items note brings the address back, ask once, in one line, whether they managed to buy <name>, then offer_choices: Bought it ("I've bought <name>") and Not yet ("I haven't bought <name> yet"). Do not infer that silence means it's bought.
4. When they say they haven't yet ("I haven't bought <name> yet"), reply in two sentences at most: no problem, their website can go live on its free Reme address in the meantime; a domain of their own is still worth having, because customers trust and remember it and Google links the site to their business name. Name no options in your words; end with offer_choices: Show the names again ("Show me the domain names again"), Use the free address ("I'll skip the domain for now and use the free address") and I'll tell you ("I'll tell you when I've bought <name>").

## Pitfalls
- Call domain_bought only once they say it's bought, never for a plan, and take their word for it.
- Never ask whether they bought it twice in a sitting.
