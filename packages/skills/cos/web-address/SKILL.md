# Web address

The website's own address: a domain the owner has, finding or buying one, and connecting it. It is a task you lead.

## When to use
- The owner mentions a domain, their web address or publishing on their own name.
- The address card was answered, Connect was pressed, or the address task is under way.

## Procedure
1. Read where it stands. The workspace's tasks hold the address task's steps, and a finished one (finished: done or declined) stays there. The open items note says where it stands.
2. While an address task is under way, speak of its steps and offer nothing new.
3. When the first build starts, the app offers the address itself with its card. If the first build is running or the website is published and there is still no address task, that offer did not land: make it in your next reply, once. Give the benefit in their terms (customers trust an address of their own, and it looks right on their quotes and cards) and that setting it up now means the website goes live on it the moment they publish, then ask whether they already have one.
4. Offer it again only when it was put off (put_off) and its put_off_until has passed.
5. Take the name from business.email in the facts, unless it is a free-mail address (gmail.com, outlook.com, yahoo.com, icloud.com and the like): then ask whether they have a domain.
6. When the owner says not now, call put_off with kind address.
7. When the owner has a domain but says the setting will have to wait (no login to hand, on their phone, later), put nothing off: the domain stays saved. Say so in your own words, with the why: their website goes live on its free address first so they can start sharing it, and the setting waits in their panel for whenever they're at a computer, a couple of minutes' work.
8. When the owner asks to see the setting for a domain being connected, call connect_domain with that name: it shows the same card again. Add only a short line.
9. A domain can be connected or bought before the website is published: it shows a coming-soon page until they publish, then goes live there on its own.

## Pitfalls
- Never write out the setting's steps yourself: the card holds them.
