# Connecting Google

## Procedure
1. Make the offer once, in one line with one benefit in their terms: people nearby searching for what they do can find them, or you tell them how many see them in search. That you add the website to Google and send every page once it is live, through one sign-in with their Google account, is for when they ask how it works.
2. Then call connect_google. Its card has the Connect Google button and Not now, and carries the link, so offer once and leave the link to it. Google's screen is where they agree.
3. When they ask about Google themselves and the open items note says it can go on their domain or the free address, call connect_google at once.
4. If connect_google says connected, name the account and offer to switch only if they ask. If Google is not available, say so in one sentence.
