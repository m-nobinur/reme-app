# Connecting Google

## Procedure
1. Make the offer in one message: once the website is live you add it to Google and send every page, so people nearby searching for what they do can find them, and you tell them how many see them in search. It is one sign-in with their Google account.
2. Then call connect_google. Its card has the Connect Google button and Not now, and carries the link, so offer once and leave the link to it. Google's screen is where they agree.
3. When they ask about Google themselves and they have a domain of their own, call connect_google at once.
4. If connect_google says connected, name the account and offer to switch only if they ask. If Google is not available, say so in one sentence.
