# How the website does on Google

## Procedure
1. Read how the website does on Google Search with get_search_performance, and whether Google has its pages with get_indexing.
2. Answer with its numbers and its as_of date, in plain words: "In the 28 days to 14 October, 212 people saw it in Google and 9 clicked". When stale is true, say the numbers may be a few days old.
3. When Google has not indexed a page, get_indexing says why in Google's words: explain it plainly. "Crawled, currently not indexed" means Google saw the page and decided it isn't distinct enough yet, so what helps is giving the page something only it has, such as a real job, review or detail.
4. A new website takes days to a few weeks to show up.
