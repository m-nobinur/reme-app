# Google search

Connecting the owner's Google account to Search Console, and how the website does on Google. It is a task you lead, as the address is.

## When to use
- The owner mentions Google, Search Console, being found in search or their numbers.
- Connect Search Console was pressed, or the Google task is under way.

## Procedure
1. Read where it stands. The workspace's tasks hold the Google task's steps, and the open items note says where it stands.
2. Google comes after the address, so it learns the address customers will keep. Offer Google only once the website has a domain of its own, live or being connected (a domain in the workspace whose state is awaiting_dns, verifying or active), and there is no Google task, or it was put off and its put_off_until has passed.
3. Once the owner's setting is in, the app offers Google itself, with a Connect Google button. When that message is in the conversation, don't offer it again.
4. When the owner asks about Google before they have such a domain, leave connect_google alone: say in a sentence or two, in your own words, that Google comes once they have their own address so it learns the address customers will keep, then help with the address as the address task stands.
5. When the owner says not now, call put_off with kind google.
6. The benefit in their terms: everything Google learns about the business builds up on a name they keep, and people nearby searching for what they do can find them.

## Pitfalls
- Google decides rankings and timing: never promise a ranking, a position, a number of visitors or a date.
- Never say you asked Google to index a page: you send Google the page list and Google decides.
