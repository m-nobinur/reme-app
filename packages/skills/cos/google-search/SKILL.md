# Google search

Connecting their Google account to Search Console, and how the website does on Google. It is a task you lead, as the address is.

## When to use
- They mention Google, Search Console, being found in search or their numbers.
- Connect Search Console was pressed, or the Google task is under way.

## Procedure
1. The open items note says where Google stands, when you last suggested it and when to bring it back; code keeps that record, so go by it. The workspace's tasks hold its steps.
2. Google comes after the address: on their own domain once it is connecting or live, or on the free address once they put the domain off or said no to one, and it moves to their domain when they connect one. Suggest it only right after they put the domain off, when the open items note brings it back, or when they ask; a Connect Search Console card waits on the stack under the chat the rest of the time.
3. Once their setting is in, or they skip the address card, the app offers Google itself, with a Connect Google button. When that message is in the conversation, don't offer it again.
4. When they ask about Google while the address is still undecided, leave connect_google alone: say in a sentence or two, in your own words, that Google comes once they have their own address so it learns the address customers will keep, then help with the address as the address task stands.
5. The benefit in their terms, one per suggestion: people nearby searching for what they do can find them, or you tell them how many see them in search.
6. When they say not now to Google, call put_off with kind google (days when they give a time) and reply with a short no problem: the card stays on the stack, so don't point at it.
7. When they say not to remind them about Google, that is a standing preference: call put_off with kind google and never true, and keep to it in every later conversation; connect it only if they ask.

## Pitfalls
- Google decides rankings and timing: never promise a ranking, a position, a number of visitors or a date.
- Never say you asked Google to index a page: you send Google the page list and Google decides.
- Never suggest Google twice in a sitting, or in the same reply as the domain.
