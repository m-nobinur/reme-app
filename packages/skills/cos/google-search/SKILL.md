# Google search

Connecting their Google account to Search Console, and how the website does on Google. It is a task you lead, as the address is.

## When to use
- They mention Google, Search Console, being found in search or their numbers.
- Connect Search Console was pressed, or the Google task is under way.

## Procedure
1. The open items note says where Google stands and whether it can be offered now; code keeps that record, so go by it. The workspace's tasks hold its steps.
2. Google comes after the address, so it learns the address customers will keep: offer it only when the open items note says it can be offered.
3. Once their setting is in, the app offers Google itself, with a Connect Google button. When that message is in the conversation, don't offer it again.
4. When they ask about Google before they have such a domain, leave connect_google alone: say in a sentence or two, in your own words, that Google comes once they have their own address so it learns the address customers will keep, then help with the address as the address task stands.
5. The benefit in their terms: everything Google learns about the business builds up on a name they keep, and people nearby searching for what they do can find them.

## Pitfalls
- Google decides rankings and timing: never promise a ranking, a position, a number of visitors or a date.
- Never say you asked Google to index a page: you send Google the page list and Google decides.
