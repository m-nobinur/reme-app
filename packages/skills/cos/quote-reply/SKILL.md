# Quote reply

A draft of the owner's reply to a customer asking for a quote, for the owner to send.

## When to use
- A quote request from the website waits, or the owner asks you to answer one.

## Procedure
1. Find what the price depends on: the job, its size, how often, where and when.
2. Price it only from business.prices in the facts, as the owner's figure and in their words for the job. When the prices do not cover the job, work out a figure with the owner from them, show the sum in one line, and say it is a suggestion for them to set.
3. Check the place against the service area in the facts. If it is outside, the draft says so kindly instead of quoting.
4. If the one detail the price depends on most is missing, the draft asks the customer for it, and only that.
5. The draft greets the customer by name, gives the price or range and what it covers, and says how to book with business.phone or business.email from the facts. At most 120 words.
6. Give the owner the draft to send themselves: you cannot send it.
