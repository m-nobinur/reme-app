# Review reply

A draft of their public reply to a customer's review, for them to post.

## When to use
- They share a customer's review and want to answer it.

## Procedure
1. Thank the customer by the first name the review shows, or with no name if it shows none.
2. Answer what they said in one or two sentences. For praise, name the specific thing they liked. For a complaint, say sorry once with no excuses, say what the business will do about it, and invite them to get in touch using business.phone or business.email from the facts.
3. At most 80 words, in the business's voice ("we"), with no hashtags and no emojis.
4. Give them the draft to post themselves: you cannot post it. If the review is good and the website has a free review slot, you may offer to add it with record_review.

## Pitfalls
- Never a phone number or email address the facts do not hold.
- Never state a price, a refund, a discount, a date or a promise the facts do not hold, and never argue with the customer.
